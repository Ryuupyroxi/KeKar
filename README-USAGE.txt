KEKAR PROPERTY MANAGER — QUICK START
====================================

KeKar keeps track of your rental properties, the people who apply to rent them,
and the pictures and notes that go with them. Everything is stored on this
drive in the KeKarData folder. Nothing is sent anywhere except the questions you
type to the KeKar assistant.


TO OPEN KEKAR
-------------
  Windows:  double-click  KeKar.bat
  Linux:    double-click  KeKar.sh   (or type  ./KeKar.sh  in a terminal)

The first time you open it, KeKar will ask for an "API key". That is a long
string of letters and numbers that the family helper gives you. Paste it in and
click Continue. You only do this once.


THE FIRST SCREEN
----------------
On the left is your list of properties. The middle shows the details of the
property you clicked. On the right is the KeKar assistant.

To add a property, click "+ Add New Property" and fill in what you know. Leave
anything blank that you are not sure about — you can come back later.


COPYING A LISTING
-----------------
Open a property, then click "Copy listing". Now open Facebook Marketplace (or
wherever you post) and paste. The words are already written for you.


THE KEKAR ASSISTANT
-------------------
Type a question in the box on the right, for example:

  "Which of my properties allow cats?"
  "Write a friendly reply to the person who asked about 123 Main St"

The assistant can read your own records, so it only knows what you have typed
into KeKar. It needs the internet to answer. If the internet is down, everything
else in KeKar still works — only the assistant goes quiet.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
Problem                          What to do
-------------------------------- ------------------------------------------
"Windows protected your PC"      Click "More info", then "Run anyway". This
                                 happens because the app is new and not yet
                                 signed. It is safe.
App opens then closes             Plug the drive in again, then retry.
Chat says it can't connect        Check the computer's internet, then retry.
Photos won't add                  Use .jpg or .png files, under 20 MB each.
Drive is slow                     Use a USB 3.0 port (usually blue), not a hub.
Forgot which drive                Look for the folder named KeKarData.
Asked to pick a folder at start   Choose the KeKarData folder on this drive.

Your work is saved as you go, and KeKar keeps a backup copy every day. If
something ever looks wrong, the family helper can restore from the KeKarData /
backups folder.
